# Narrated native Blender cutaway films

1. Continue the existing Blender project when that is the requested medium. Make a FULL_COPY scene and copy shared materials before changing geometry, animation or shading; preserve the delivered source blend.
2. Plan the explanatory sequence before keyframing: full exterior orbit, opening, viewing mechanism, exposure mechanism, output. Use per-component time slots, synthesize actual Azure speech, measure each clip and fit with bounded pitch-preserving speed adjustment. Keep the original and fitted audio plus actual text/timing JSON; do not narrate unrelated phases merely to meet a target duration.
3. Teach each component with three synchronized cues: a light head/path arriving at the component, a local selection outline and a projected adjacent label. Derive label anchors from real animated geometry through the active camera matrix, not guessed screen coordinates. Keep labels away from the active object and readable at delivery resolution.
4. Bake native object/camera/data properties to absolute frames. Use keyed curve bevel_factor_end for progressive paths and keyed geometry for mechanical movement. For DSLRs, test that the exposure path does not reach shutter height until all shutter blades are clear and the mirror has risen. Distinguish optical rays from electrical signals visually and in narration.
5. Verify a 360 orbit numerically by unwrapping camera azimuth across the orbit interval; two different screenshots do not prove a complete turn. Check the starting and ending camera positions and actual rendered front/rear samples.
6. Benchmark a short animation, not only one still, before committing render resolution/samples. Eevee transmission needs renderer-appropriate refraction settings; a controlled Transparent/Principled mix can make an explicitly educational visualization readable, but is not measured optical glass or path-traced refraction.
7. Launch a long GUI render with bpy.ops.render.render('INVOKE_DEFAULT', animation=True), retain the returned started status, and monitor actual numbered PNGs externally. Do not claim completion from RUNNING_MODAL. Save with MCP autostart disabled before launch.
8. A PNG filename can exist while Blender is writing it. Read immutable bytes and require the complete PNG IEND trailer before decoding; also retry OSError, SyntaxError, ValueError and EOFError within a bounded timeout. Do not enable truncated-image loading or replace missing frames with duplicates.
9. Write timing metadata through the external MCP client receipt, not bpy.data.texts.new: safe mode can forbid text datablocks as an execution path. Likewise measure elapsed time in the client; the safe-mode whitelist can reject import time despite its being stdlib. Never disable the safety filter to make instrumentation work.
10. Stream annotated RGB frames into FFmpeg with explicit frame rate, dimensions, video codec and the measured narration track. Preserve a master and a smaller same-resolution chat copy when needed. Verify the actual delivery derivative, not only the master.
11. For a requested model replacement, acquire and inspect an actual licensed mesh before editing; previews and material-only edits do not satisfy replacement. Keep the approved teaching animation in a full-copy scene, register the new shell to its optical axis, and compare real front/rear/exploded renders. Preserve source license boundaries: a royalty-free render license may not permit sharing the stock model or editable asset bundle.
12. Prefer a data-only GLB interchange when MCP safe mode forbids external .blend datablock loading; do not disable its protection. Export with autoexecution disabled, validate the GLB header/JSON and re-import through an allowed importer. Normalize using measured world-space model dimensions: source/export/import scene unit scales can multiply geometry unexpectedly. Rebuild unsupported procedural shaders and explicitly restore planar LCD UVs after interchange; inspect the actual new assembly bounding box before rendering a film.
13. When comparing animations in multiple Blender scenes, activate each scene, set its frame, update its view layer and snapshot values before switching to the other. Inactive-scene object properties can stay at a previous frame and produce false differences. Compare exact production camera and optical transforms across all frames, while allowing the intended shell/visibility changes.
14. Reopen the saved blend independently with automatic Python disabled and a nonzero --python-exit-code for failures. Check geometry, orbit span, component motion, optical gates, absence of drivers and autostart. Fully decode the final MP4, verify A/V duration and frame count, correlate decoded audio with its Azure source, check clipping and inspect sampled final frames including the ending.
