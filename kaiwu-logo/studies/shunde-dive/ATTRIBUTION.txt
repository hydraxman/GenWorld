Revision 3:
The camera ends centered on Shunde's north entrance.
The clean 3D background is recorded to a separate video, then stylized
locally into a darker painterly/hand-drawn illustration. The metallic
badge is composited afterward without applying the background treatment
to its surfaces. A separate alpha-preserving ProRes 4444 badge pass is
also retained locally. Stylization does not make the approximate
architecture or corridor dimensions a surveyed reconstruction.
